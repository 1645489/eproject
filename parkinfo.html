Opening Hours

We are open all week from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM (Monday – Sunday).

Special events may have extended hours. Please check our announcements or social media pages.

We ensure a clean and safe environment for our guests with full-day lifeguard assistance and family-friendly facilities.

Why Visit Us?

Sanjalika Water Park offers world-class water slides, pools, and family attractions. Enjoy a fun-filled day with secure facilities, lifeguards, and a variety of food options.

Our park is designed to provide maximum comfort, safety, and enjoyment for visitors of all ages.

Location

Sanjalika Water Park, Main Super Highway, Karachi, Pakistan

Located just outside Karachi city, easily accessible via the main Super Highway.

Tickets & Policies

  • General Admission: PKR 1,000 per person
  • Children under 3 years: Free Entry
  • Group discounts available for schools & corporates
  • Outside food not allowed
  • Please bring proper swimwear for water slides

Enjoy unlimited access to all attractions for the whole day with your ticket. Discounts for group bookings available.
